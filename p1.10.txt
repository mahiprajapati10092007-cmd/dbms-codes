P1.10  Temperature Converter   [Easy]  [Arithmetic, ROUND, operator precedence] Problem Statement:
Declare v_celsius NUMBER. Convert to Fahrenheit using F = C*9/5 + 32 and to Kelvin using K = C + 273.
15. Print all three values, rounding Fahrenheit to 1 decimal. Then reverse: take v_f NUMBER :
= 98.6 and convert back to Celsius, verify you get 37. Observe: what happens if you write C*9/5+32
without brackets vs C*(9/5)+32 — is the result different? Why or why not?




DECLARE
    v_roll NUMBER := 34;
    v_celsius NUMBER;
    v_fahrenheit NUMBER;
    v_kelvin NUMBER;
    v_f NUMBER := 98.6;
    v_c2 NUMBER;
BEGIN
    v_celsius := MOD(v_roll, 20) + 25;

    v_fahrenheit := ROUND(
        v_celsius * 9 / 5 + 32,
        1
    );

    v_kelvin := v_celsius + 273.15;

    v_c2 := (v_f - 32) * 5 / 9;

    DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE(
        'Celsius: ' || v_celsius
    );
    DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE(
        'Fahrenheit: ' || v_fahrenheit
    );
    DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE(
        'Kelvin: ' || v_kelvin
    );
    DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE(
        '98.6 F in Celsius: ' ||
        ROUND(v_c2, 1)
    );
END;
