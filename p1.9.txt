P1.9  Torrent Power Electricity Bill   [Easy]  [Variables, arithmetic, ROUND, concatenation]
Problem Statement:  Build a simple electricity bill for Torrent Power. Declare: v_units NUMBER,
c_rate CONSTANT NUMBER := 5 (Rs. per unit), c_fixed CONSTANT NUMBER := 70 (fixed monthly charge).
Calculate: energy_charge = units * rate, total = energy_charge + fixed charge. Print a mini bill:
consumer name (your name), units consumed, energy charge, fixed charge, and total, each on its
own line using DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE.






DECLARE
    v_name VARCHAR2(50) := 'Mahi Prajapati';
    v_roll NUMBER := 34;

    v_units NUMBER;
    c_rate CONSTANT NUMBER := 5;
    c_fixed CONSTANT NUMBER := 70;

    v_energy NUMBER;
    v_total NUMBER;
BEGIN
    v_units := v_roll * 10 + 50;
    v_energy := v_units * c_rate;
    v_total := v_energy + c_fixed;

    DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('Consumer: ' || v_name);
    DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE('Units: ' || v_units);
    DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE(
        'Energy Charge: Rs.' || v_energy
    );
    DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE(
        'Fixed Charge: Rs.' || c_fixed
    );
    DBMS_OUTPUT.PUT_LINE(
        'Total: Rs.' || v_total
    );
END;
